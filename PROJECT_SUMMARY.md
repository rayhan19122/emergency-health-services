# Emergency Healthcare Access Platform

## The idea
A single app that helps people find and request critical healthcare services during an emergency — when every minute counts and calling around to hospitals one by one isn't an option. Instead of searching blindly, patients can see what's actually available right now, compare prices, and request it on the spot.

## What it does for patients
The platform brings together **four emergency services** in one place:

- **Emergency Beds** — Browse hospitals, see which beds are available and at what price, and book one.
- **Ambulance** — Find nearby operators, pick the type of vehicle, get a fare estimate, and request a pickup.
- **Blood Bank** — Search by blood type, check how many units are in stock, and request blood.
- **Diagnostic Tests** — Search for a test by name, compare prices across hospitals, and call or book directly.

Patients also get their own booking history, live status updates on every request, and can attach a prescription where one is needed. Prices are shown in Bangladeshi Taka.

## What it does for providers
Hospitals, blood banks, and ambulance operators get their own admin views to manage their side of things:

- Update availability, pricing, and inventory (beds, blood stock, fleet, test catalog).
- Receive incoming patient requests in real time and approve, reject, confirm, or complete them.
- See live dashboards showing pending, confirmed, and closed requests at a glance.

Each provider only sees and manages **their own service** — a bed manager can't touch blood bank data, and so on.

## Who runs it
A **platform administrator** oversees everything: adding and organizing hospitals and operators, managing user accounts and their roles, and stepping in when needed. Administrators can suspend access or retire an organization without ever losing historical records — past bookings stay intact.

## Why it matters
In a real emergency, families lose precious time trying to track down an available bed, a unit of blood, or an ambulance. This platform replaces that scramble with a single, trustworthy place to check availability and act immediately — making emergency healthcare faster to reach and easier to coordinate for everyone involved.

## Where it stands
The project is a working, actively developed application with all four services in place, live coordination between patients and providers, and recent additions like printable receipts. It's built as a complete capstone/final-year software project, with supporting documentation and setup guides.
